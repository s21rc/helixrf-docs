    Manual · Helix SSPA Controller

    Helix SSPA Controller¶

    Control and protection for solid-state HF amplifiers. The controller pairs an STM32G474 control board with an ESP32-P4 touch display. It switches bands automatically from your radio, sequences the RX/TX relay and the PA bias, and trips in hardware on SWR, filter reflection, output power, temperature, voltage and current.

    This manual covers controller board Rev 4.01 with firmware 105 (controller and display). Use the version menu at the top to read an older edition.

    RF safety

    High RF power. Never operate without a proper load or antenna connected.

    What it does¶

    • Protection in hardware. Fast comparators trip on antenna SWR, LPF SWR, output power, PA temperature, supply voltage and drain current. A latch holds the fault, removes the bias, opens the PA supply switch, inhibits the radio and pulls its ALC down until you reset it.
    • Safe TX sequencing. The RX/TX relay closes first, the bias follows 20 ms later, and on release the bias goes before the relay moves back. A PTT held at power-up or after a fault never keys the amplifier.
    • Automatic band switching from band voltage, BCD, CAT or Icom CI-V, with per-band LPF and antenna maps.
    • Metering: SSB-safe peak-hold PEP and SWR, average power, supply voltage and current, two temperatures and fan speed, on four meter faces.
    • Accessories: 4-way antenna selector with a separate RX antenna, antenna rotator with GS-232B network control, and control of compatible power supplies over CAN.
    • Remote: a browser app on your phone or PC, and an optional second display (remote head) on your network. Firmware updates for both boards over WiFi.

    System overview¶

    The controller sits at the centre of the amplifier: it switches the RF path, biases the pallet, watches SWR, temperature, voltage and current, and talks to the radio, the display and the optional modules. Numbered blocks are explained in the notes on the diagram.

    Download the block diagram (PDF)

    The High Side Switch, the high-voltage NMOS switch and the RF sense module are separate HelixRF modules with their own manuals. This manual covers how they connect to the controller.
