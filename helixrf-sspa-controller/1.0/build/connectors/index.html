    Connectors and wiring¶

    Every connector on the controller board, what it does, its pin-out and how to wire it. Pin numbers follow the board silkscreen; pin 1 is GND on almost every connector.

    RF safety

    High RF power. Never operate without a proper load or antenna connected.

    Two footprints per connector

    Most connectors appear twice on the board under two reference numbers, for example J3 / J4. The first is a JST PH (2.0 mm) side-entry socket, the second is a row of solder pads wired in parallel. Use whichever suits your build: a crimped JST PH lead, or wires soldered straight to the pads. Never use both at once.

    Power¶

    J22 / J23 · 12 V power in¶

    The 12 V logic supply for the controller, the display, the fan and all relay outputs.

    Pin Signal Direction Notes
    1 GND
    2 +12 V In 12–14 V DC (13.8 V is fine). Do not exceed 15 V.

    The input is protected by a 5 A resettable fuse (F1) and a reverse-polarity diode: connected the wrong way round, the fuse trips and the board stays off. Size the supply for the board and display plus your fan and every relay coil that can be on at the same time (one LPF relay, up to four antenna relays and the RX/TX relay).

     12 V supply                       Controller J22
 ┌─────────┐                       ┌──────────┐
 │    +12V ├───────────────────────┤ 2  +12V  │
 │     GND ├───────────────────────┤ 1  GND   │
 └─────────┘                       └──────────┘

    Warning

    The 52 V PA supply never goes to the controller board. It runs through the High Side Switch module (see the Power diagram above).

    J39 / J40 · LPF module power¶

    Supplies 12 V to the LPF relay board. Two pins per rail share the current.

    Pin Signal Direction
    1, 2 GND
    3, 4 +12 V Out 
     Controller J39                     LPF module
 ┌──────────┐                      ┌──────────┐
 │ 3,4 +12V ├──────────────────────┤ +12V     │
 │ 1,2 GND  ├──────────────────────┤ GND      │
 └──────────┘                      └──────────┘

    J8 / J9 · PSU monitor¶

    Voltage and current readings from the High Side Switch module, shown on the display and used by the protection. Pin 4 powers the sensor.

    Pin Signal Direction Notes
    1 GND
    2 V_SENSE In PA supply voltage, scaled by the module
    3 I_SENSE In PA current: shunt amplifier, Hall sensor or BTS current-sense output
    4 +3.3 V Out Supply for the sensor

    The sensor type (shunt, Hall or BTS) and its values are set on the display. See Power module.

     Controller J8                      High Side Switch (sense)
 ┌───────────────┐                 ┌─────────────────┐
 │ 4  +3.3V      ├─────────────────┤ sensor supply   │
 │ 3  I_SENSE    │◄────────────────┤ current out     │
 │ 2  V_SENSE    │◄────────────────┤ voltage out     │
 │ 1  GND        ├─────────────────┤ GND             │
 └───────────────┘                 └─────────────────┘

    J10 / J11 · PSU protection¶

    Turns the 52 V PA supply off when the hardware protection latches a fault. Pins 2 and 3 are open-drain outputs (MOSFET to GND) with opposite sense, so the switch module can use whichever its gate driver needs.

    Pin Signal Direction Normal operation Fault latched
    1 GND
    2 ~PROT-VCC Out Open Switched to GND
    3 PROT-VCC Out Switched to GND Open
    4 +3.3 V Out Supply for the switch logic
    • Use pin 3 for a switch that stays on only while its control input is held to GND. This is fail-safe: a broken wire turns the PA supply off.
    • Use pin 2 for a switch that turns off when its control input is pulled to GND.

    The High Side Switch manual shows which pin it uses.

     Controller J10                     High Side Switch (control)
 ┌───────────────┐                 ┌─────────────────┐
 │ 4  +3.3V      ├─────────────────┤ logic supply    │
 │ 3  PROT-VCC   ├────────────────►│ enable / kill   │
 │ 2  ~PROT-VCC  ├────────────────►│ (use one)       │
 │ 1  GND        ├─────────────────┤ GND             │
 └───────────────┘                 └─────────────────┘

    PA¶

    J30 / J31 · PA bias¶

    Switches the pallet's gate bias. The controller applies bias only while transmitting, after the RX/TX relay has settled (20 ms), and removes it 5 ms before the relay returns to receive. A protection fault removes it at once, in hardware.

    Pin Signal Direction Notes
    1 GND
    2 BIAS-IN In Bias supply from the pallet, up to 12 V
    3 BIAS-OUT Out Switched bias back to the pallet's gate-bias input
    4 Gate kill (optional) Out Open drain: pulled to GND while a fault is latched
    5 +12 V Out Low-current supply for an external pinch-off circuit
    6 −12 V Out Low-current supply for an external pinch-off circuit
    7 PINCH Out Logic high (3.3 V) while a fault is latched
    8 BIAS_CTRL Out Logic high while transmitting, for pallets with a bias-enable input

    Most LDMOS pallets need only pins 1–3: break the pallet's bias supply line and route it through BIAS-IN and BIAS-OUT. Pins 4–8 are for pallets that need a hard gate kill, a negative pinch-off voltage or a separate bias-enable signal.

     Controller J30                     RF pallet
 ┌───────────────┐                 ┌──────────────────┐
 │ 2  BIAS-IN    │◄────────────────┤ bias supply out  │
 │ 3  BIAS-OUT   ├────────────────►│ gate bias in     │
 │ 4  Gate kill  ├ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─►│ gate (optional)  │
 │ 8  BIAS_CTRL  ├ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─►│ bias enable (opt)│
 │ 1  GND        ├─────────────────┤ GND              │
 └───────────────┘                 └──────────────────┘
       ─── required    ─ ─ optional

    RF sensing and thermal¶

    J3 / J4 and J5 / J6 · SWR bridges¶

    DC outputs from the two directional couplers. J3 / J4 reads the bridge on the antenna side (after the LPF). J5 / J6 reads the bridge between the PA and the LPF, which catches a wrong or failed filter. Both feed the hardware SWR trips; the antenna bridge also drives the power and SWR meters.

    Pin Signal Direction Notes
    1 GND
    2 Reflected (ANT-R / LPF-R) In DC from the reflected detector
    3 GND
    4 Forward (ANT-F / LPF-F) In DC from the forward detector

    Use shielded cable or a twisted pair per signal, and keep it away from the PA output coax.

     SWR bridge                         Controller J3 (antenna) / J5 (LPF)
 ┌──────────────┐                  ┌──────────────┐
 │ FWD detector ├─────────────────►│ 4  FWD       │
 │          GND ├──────────────────┤ 3  GND       │
 │ REF detector ├─────────────────►│ 2  REF       │
 │          GND ├──────────────────┤ 1  GND       │
 └──────────────┘                  └──────────────┘

    Calibrate the meter after wiring; see RF power calibration.

    J28 / J29 · NTC temperature sensors¶

    Two NTC thermistors. The fan speed follows whichever reads higher. The hardware over-temperature trip watches NTC 1 only, so mount NTC 1 on the pallet heatsink, next to the transistor. NTC 2 can go on the LPF, the combiner or the PSU.

    Each NTC sits between +3.3 V and its sense pin; the board has a 10 kΩ resistor from each sense pin to GND.

    Pin Signal Direction Notes
    1 +3.3 V Out NTC 1 supply
    2 NTC-1 In NTC 1 sense (heatsink; used by the hardware trip)
    3 +3.3 V Out NTC 2 supply
    4 NTC-2 In NTC 2 sense

    Use 10 kΩ NTCs. The firmware defaults to 10 kΩ at 25 °C and β = 3950, the values of the common 10 kΩ "3950" thermistor. For a different NTC, enter its resistance and beta on the display; see Temperature and fan.

     Controller J28
 ┌──────────────┐        NTC 1 (pallet heatsink)
 │ 1  +3.3V     ├────────/\/\/\────┐
 │ 2  NTC-1     ├──────────────────┘
 │              │        NTC 2
 │ 3  +3.3V     ├────────/\/\/\────┐
 │ 4  NTC-2     ├──────────────────┘
 └──────────────┘

    J20 / J21 · Fan¶

    Variable fan speed. The speed ramps from 10 % at the "fan low" temperature to 100 % at "fan high"; the fan never stops completely. Two-wire fans are switched on their negative lead; four-wire PC fans take the PWM signal.

    Pin Signal Direction Notes
    1 GND
    2 PWM Out PWM control for 4-wire fans
    3 FAN − Out Switched negative for 2-wire fans
    4 +12 V Out Fan supply (filtered) 
     Controller J20                 Fan
 ┌──────────────┐              ┌──────────┐
 │ 4  +12V      ├──────────────┤ + (red)  │
 │ 3  FAN −     ├──────────────┤ − (black)│
 └──────────────┘              └──────────┘

     Controller J20                 Fan
 ┌──────────────┐              ┌──────────────┐
 │ 4  +12V      ├──────────────┤ +12V (yellow)│
 │ 1  GND       ├──────────────┤ GND  (black) │
 │ 2  PWM       ├──────────────┤ PWM  (blue)  │
 └──────────────┘              └──────────────┘
   (tach wire not used)

    If the fan runs backwards to the temperature (fast when cold), turn on Invert PWM on the Temperature page.

    Band and antenna switching¶

    J41 / J42 and J43 / J44 · LPF band select¶

    Eight band outputs to the LPF relay board, one per filter bank. Exactly one output is on at a time. Which bank each band uses is set in the LPF map. The outputs are given twice, so you can use whichever matches your relay board:

    • J43 / J44 (LPF): sourcing. The active pin goes to +12 V. Wire each relay coil between the pin and GND.
    • J41 / J42 (~LPF): sinking. The active pin goes to GND (open collector). Wire each relay coil between +12 V and the pin.
    Pin J43 / J44 (sourcing) J41 / J42 (sinking)
    1 BAND1 ~BAND1
    2 BAND2 ~BAND2
    3 BAND3 ~BAND3
    4 BAND4 ~BAND4
    5 BAND5 ~BAND5
    6 BAND6 ~BAND6
    7 BAND7 ~BAND7
    8 BAND8 ~BAND8

    The drivers (A2982 sourcing, ULN2803A sinking) are rated 500 mA per output; keep each relay coil well below that. The sinking outputs have clamp diodes on the board. For the sourcing outputs, fit a flyback diode across each relay coil on the LPF board.

     Controller J43                 LPF module (one bank shown)
 ┌──────────────┐              ┌────────────────────────┐
 │ 1  BAND1     ├─────────────►│ relay coil ──┐         │
 │ …            │              │              GND       │
 │ 8  BAND8     ├─────────────►│ (one per bank)         │
 └──────────────┘              └────────────────────────┘
 Power the LPF board from J39 (+12 V and GND).

     Controller J41                 LPF module (one bank shown)
 ┌──────────────┐              ┌────────────────────────┐
 │ 1  ~BAND1    ├──────────────┤ relay coil ── +12V     │
 │ …            │              │                        │
 │ 8  ~BAND8    ├──────────────┤ (one per bank)         │
 └──────────────┘              └────────────────────────┘
 Relay +12 V comes from J39.

    J45 / J46 · Antenna selector¶

    Drives the optional 4-way antenna selector and a separate receive-antenna relay. All outputs are sourcing (+12 V when on, A2982 driver, 500 mA per output). The TX antenna is chosen per band on the display and never changes while transmitting.

    Pin Signal Notes
    1 ANT1 TX antenna relay 1
    2 ANT2 TX antenna relay 2
    3 ANT3 TX antenna relay 3
    4 ANT4 Dedicated RX antenna relay
    5–8 EX1–EX4 Spare sourcing outputs, not used by firmware 105

    The TX antenna relays use a cumulative code, so a cascade of three changeover relays selects four antennas:

    Antenna ANT1 ANT2 ANT3
    ANT 1 (default, no relay on) off off off
    ANT 2 on off off
    ANT 3 on on off
    ANT 4 on on on

    The RX antenna relay (ANT4) is on only while receiving, with PA ENABLE on, on a band set to use the RX antenna. It drops the moment the amplifier keys.

    J45 has no GND pin. Return the relay coils to GND at the selector board, taking GND from J39 or the chassis. Fit a flyback diode across each coil.

     Controller J45                     Antenna selector board
 ┌──────────────┐                  ┌─────────────────────────┐
 │ 1  ANT1      ├─────────────────►│ K1 coil ─┐              │
 │ 2  ANT2      ├─────────────────►│ K2 coil ─┤  coils to    │
 │ 3  ANT3      ├─────────────────►│ K3 coil ─┤  GND         │
 │ 4  ANT4 (RX) ├─────────────────►│ RX relay─┘              │
 └──────────────┘                  └─────────────────────────┘

    Radio interface¶

    J26 / J27 · PTT and RX/TX relay¶

    Keying input from the radio and the drive for the RX/TX changeover relay. The controller sequences the relay and the bias so the relay never switches with RF present.

    Pin Signal Direction Notes
    1 GND
    2 ~PTT In Active low: radio's TX-GND / SEND output pulls it to GND
    3 nRST-RLY Out Open drain: pulled to GND for 300 ms when you press PA RESET
    4 RX/TX RLY Out +12 V while transmitting; flyback diode on board

    nRST-RLY resets external modules that latch their own faults. Wire it to the module's reset input (active low), or leave it unconnected.

     Radio                Controller J26              RX/TX relay
 ┌──────────┐        ┌──────────────┐            ┌──────────┐
 │ SEND/PTT ├───────►│ 2  ~PTT      │            │          │
 │      GND ├────────┤ 1  GND       ├────────────┤ coil −   │
 └──────────┘        │ 4  RX/TX RLY ├───────────►│ coil +   │
                     └──────────────┘            └──────────┘

    Test before going on the air

    Check that the relay changes over on PTT with no RF applied, before you connect the drive cable.

    J24 / J25 · ALC and TX inhibit¶

    Protection outputs to the radio. While a fault is latched, the controller pulls the radio's ALC negative and asserts TX inhibit, so the radio drops its drive. In normal operation the ALC pins sit at 0 V; they do not regulate power.

    Pin Signal Direction Notes
    1 GND
    2 ALC (4 V range) Out 0 V normally, about −4 V on a fault
    3 ALC (10 V range) Out 0 V normally, about −10 V on a fault
    4 TX-INH Out Open drain: switched to GND on a fault

    Use the ALC pin that matches your radio's ALC input range: −4 V for most current radios, −10 V for older ones. Check your radio's manual. Wire TX-INH only if your radio has a TX-inhibit input that disables transmit when pulled to GND.

     Controller J24                     Radio
 ┌──────────────┐                  ┌───────────────┐
 │ 2 or 3  ALC  ├─────────────────►│ ALC in        │
 │ 4  TX-INH    ├─────────────────►│ TX inhibit in │
 │ 1  GND       ├──────────────────┤ GND           │
 └──────────────┘                  └───────────────┘

    J16 / J17 · CAT / CI-V¶

    Serial link to the radio for automatic band switching by CAT (two-wire serial) or Icom CI-V (single wire). The logic level is 5 V or 3.3 V, set by solder jumper JP2; match it to your radio or interface. Pin 4 powers a level converter (RS-232 or USB) if you use one.

    Pin Signal Direction Notes
    1 GND
    2 CAT-RX In Data from the radio (CAT only)
    3 CAT-TX Out / both Data to the radio. In CI-V mode this is the single CI-V line.
    4 +5 V Out Supply for the CAT module 
     Radio / CAT module             Controller J16
 ┌───────────────┐             ┌──────────────┐
 │ TXD           ├────────────►│ 2  CAT-RX    │
 │ RXD           │◄────────────┤ 3  CAT-TX    │
 │ GND           ├─────────────┤ 1  GND       │
 └───────────────┘             └──────────────┘

     Radio (CI-V jack)              Controller J16
 ┌───────────────┐             ┌──────────────┐
 │ tip (CI-V)    ├─────────────┤ 3  CAT-TX    │
 │ sleeve (GND)  ├─────────────┤ 1  GND       │
 └───────────────┘             └──────────────┘

    Warning

    Most radios' RS-232 ports use ±12 V levels. Do not connect them directly: use a level converter powered from pin 4.

    Display and accessories¶

    J18 / J19 · Display¶

    Power and the serial link to the ESP32-P4 touch display. The display also passes firmware updates to the controller over this link.

    Pin Signal Direction Notes
    1 GND
    2 RX In 3.3 V UART, from display TX
    3 TX Out 3.3 V UART, to display RX
    4 +5 V Out Display supply 
     Controller J18                     Display
 ┌──────────────┐                  ┌──────────────┐
 │ 4  +5V       ├─────────────────►│ +5V          │
 │ 3  TX        ├─────────────────►│ RX           │
 │ 2  RX        │◄─────────────────┤ TX           │
 │ 1  GND       ├──────────────────┤ GND          │
 └──────────────┘                  └──────────────┘

    TX goes to RX

    Controller TX (pin 3) goes to the display's RX, and controller RX (pin 2) to the display's TX. Some display manufacturers label their connector the other way round, so check the pin-out in your display's manual. See Display and controller not talking.

    The display's own CAN port connects to the power supply's CAN bus, where fitted (see the "Other I/O" diagram and CAN bus).

    J32 / J33 · Rotator¶

    Antenna rotator control: two relay outputs and the position potentiometer. Supports 360° and 450° rotators with 3-point calibration.

    Pin Board label Function Notes
    1 GND
    2 ROT-V Position pot wiper (in) 0 to about 14 V
    3 ROT-L CW relay (out) Open drain, switched to GND, 30 V max
    4 ROT-R CCW relay (out) Open drain, switched to GND, 30 V max

    Pins 3 and 4

    On Rev 4.01 the board labels are crossed: pin 3 (ROT-L) turns the rotator clockwise (the >> button), pin 4 (ROT-R) counter-clockwise (<<). Wire by function, not by label. If the rotator turns the wrong way on >>, swap the two wires.

    The pot needs its reference voltage from the rotator control box: connect the pot's ends to the box's reference and GND, and the wiper to ROT-V. The calibration works with the pot wired either way round.

     Controller J32                     Rotator control box
 ┌──────────────┐                  ┌───────────────────────┐
 │ 3  ROT-L (CW)├──────────────────┤ CW relay coil ── +V   │
 │ 4  ROT-R(CCW)├──────────────────┤ CCW relay coil ── +V  │
 │ 2  ROT-V     │◄─────────────────┤ pot wiper             │
 │ 1  GND       ├──────────────────┤ GND / pot low end     │
 └──────────────┘                  └───────────────────────┘

    J34 · USB-C¶

    USB 2.0 port on the controller board, for service only. It is not needed in normal use: firmware updates go through the display (see Firmware). Use it to recover a controller with STM32CubeProgrammer over USB DFU; see Recovering the controller.

    Expansion and service connectors¶

    These connectors are not needed for a normal build.

    Connector Name Pins Use
    J14 / J15 FREQ-CNT 1 GND · 2 LVDS+ · 3 LVDS− · 4 PROT-DRIVE (in) Pin 4 is an external drive-overload fault input (3.3 V logic, high = fault); it trips the RF IN fault. The LVDS pair is reserved for a frequency counter.
    J12 / J13 I2C 1 GND · 2 SCL · 3 SDA · 4 +3.3 V Reserved for expansion; on the same bus as the settings EEPROM
    J35 / J36 NEURAL 1 GND · 2 RX · 3 TX · 4 +5 V For the HelixRF Neural module (3.3 V UART)
    J37 / J38 OTA 1 GND · 2 EXT-IO1 · 3 BOOT0 · 4 nRST Boot-mode and reset lines for an external programmer or module. Not needed for updates.
    J7 SWD 1 +3.3 V · 2 SWDIO · 3 GND · 4 SWCLK · 5 SWO · 6 nRST STM32 programmer / debugger (2×3, 2.54 mm)
